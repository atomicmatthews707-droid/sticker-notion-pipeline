## Style
a sticker sheet of ten different die-cut stickers, each with its own funny, sarcastic, original joke written by you,
bold high-contrast viral typography, black and white graphics with a thick clean white die-cut border around each sticker,
on a plain light beige background

## Composition
ten stickers arranged neatly with space between them, every sticker a different format and design, no repeated punch lines,
no cartoons or illustrations, all text large, legible and spelled correctly

## Avoid
real people or celebrities, brand logos, film or TV characters, misspelled words, repeated jokes
