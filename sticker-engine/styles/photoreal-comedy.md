## Style
cinematic, ultra-high-definition, ultra-realistic 4K photograph, crisp depth of field, dramatic lighting

## Composition
square 1:1 frame, one clear focal subject

## Text
witty caption as a bold, high-contrast text overlay, large and fully legible, spelled exactly as written in the prompt

## Avoid
recognisable real people or celebrities, brand logos, film or TV characters, watermarks, blurry or warped faces

## Notes
Humour is irreverent and PG-13: sarcasm and innuendo are fine; nothing sexually explicit, hateful, or aimed at a real person.
