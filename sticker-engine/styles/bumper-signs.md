# Bumper and street signs
cutout: floodfill
background: white
qa: sticker
guard: strict
aspect: 1:1

<!-- Preset B. Traffic-sign-shaped stickers in bold black and yellow with a distressed metal look and satirical warning
     text. The sign sits on a plain white background so the cutout can lift it cleanly. -->

## Style
traffic-sign-shaped sticker (diamond warning sign, shield or rectangular street sign), bold black and yellow print, distressed weathered metal texture, satirical warning text

## Composition
the sign centered and fully visible with a margin around it

## Text
short, bold, high-contrast sign lettering, spelled exactly as written in the prompt

## Palette
black and warning yellow

## Avoid
real road-sign wording that could be mistaken for an official instruction, brand logos, real people, watermarks

## Notes
Signs are jokes, not instructions. Satire and innuendo are fine; nothing hateful or sexually explicit.
